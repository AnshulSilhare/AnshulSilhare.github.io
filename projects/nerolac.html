Internship Case Study

Nerolac Nerofix: Logistics Benchmarking & Inventory Optimization

RoleSupply Chain Analytics Intern
OrganizationNerofix Private Limited (A subsidiary of Kansai Nerolac Paints Ltd.)
TimelineMay 2026 - July 2026 · 2 Months
Tech StackPython (Pandas, NumPy), SQL, Power BI (DAX), Advanced Excel (VBA)

Situation

Kansai Nerolac Nerofix operates a national distribution network distributing industrial and retail adhesives across 7 regional hubs. The supply chain team faced two core operational challenges:

  • Outbound Logistics: Transporter performance was reviewed through manual meetings without a standardized scoring model. High-volume carriers were difficult to compare fairly against low-volume ones, and delivery delays were treated solely as transit issues without measuring internal warehouse processing time.
  • Depot Inventory Planning: Inventory replenishment relied on flat lead-time assumptions across all locations rather than actual factory-to-depot transit distances. This led to unnecessary safety stock buffers, occasional stockout risks on fast-moving items, and undetected slow-moving inventory.

Task

I was tasked with leading two core analytical initiatives aligned with the supply chain and logistics function:

  • Transporter Scorecard & SLA Breach Analysis: Build an objective, composite scoring framework to benchmark 66 freight carriers across national routes and isolate root causes of delivery SLA breaches.
  • Dynamic Inventory Replenishment & Stockout Analytics: Design a demand-driven inventory model across all 7 regional depots (450+ SKUs) to calculate dynamic Safety Stock (SS) and Reorder Points (ROP) based on factory-specific lead times, ABC classification (80/15/5 revenue share), and FSN movement velocity (Fast, Slow, Non-moving).

Action

To execute this, I implemented a structured five-step analytical workflow:

  • 1. ERP Data Extraction & Cleaning: Extracted and validated 13 months of transactional dispatch records (8,450+ shipments across 66 carriers). Handled missing timestamps and cleaned delivery logs using Python (Pandas) to build a consolidated data foundation.
  • 2. Cycle Time & SLA Root-Cause Analysis: Deconstructed the order-to-delivery cycle into internal processing vs. road transit. Identified that warehouse processing accounted for 57% of total cycle time (5.40 days avg), while actual road transit took 43% (4.02 days avg), proving that internal delays consumed the delivery SLA buffer.
  • 3. Log-Scaled Carrier Scorecard (Power BI): Designed a normalized composite scoring model in Power BI (DAX) to evaluate carriers fairly without volume bias:
    $$\begin{aligned} \text{Composite Score} &= (0.60 \times \text{On-Time \%}) + (0.20 \times \text{Near-Miss \%}) \\ &\quad + \left(0.20 \times \frac{\ln(1 + \text{Shipments})}{\ln(1 + \text{Max Shipments})} \times 100\right) \end{aligned}$$
    Weights: 60% On-Time SLA, 20% Near-Miss compliance, and 20% Log-Scaled dispatch volume normalization to prevent low-volume bias.
    Built a 3-page interactive Power BI dashboard and operational Excel sheets covering national rankings, SLA breach trends, and route-level carrier drill-downs.
  • 4. Dynamic Safety Stock & ROP Modeling: Replaced flat assumptions with factory-to-depot lead-time matrices and applied Pareto ABC (80/15/5 revenue share) and FSN movement classification (Fast, Slow, Non-moving):
    $$\begin{aligned} \text{Safety Stock (SS)} &= Z \times \sigma_d \times \sqrt{L} \\[5pt] \text{Reorder Point (ROP)} &= (D_{\text{avg}} \times L) + \text{SS} \end{aligned}$$
    Where \(Z\) is the Service Level factor (Z-score), \(\sigma_d\) is daily demand volatility (standard deviation), \(L\) is factory-to-hub lead time in days, and \(D_{\text{avg}}\) is average daily demand.
  • 5. Operational Deployment (Excel VBA): Automated the analytical models into formatted, macro-enabled Excel workbooks (VBA)—delivering dedicated operational Excel sheets and inventory replenishment tools with dynamic live formulas. This enabled depot managers and logistics teams across all 7 regional hubs to review carrier SLAs and recalculate reorder triggers without needing to execute code.

Result

Quantifiable Outcomes & Business Impact
  • Carrier Benchmarking & Route Optimization: Evaluated 66 transporters across 8,450+ dispatches and identified 4 immediate route-level carrier reallocations on active lanes to improve on-time compliance without adding new vendors.
  • SLA Bottleneck Identification: Isolated internal warehouse processing (5.40 days) as the primary contributor to SLA breaches, providing actionable insights to streamline order-approval workflows.
  • Replenishment Precision: At South Hub 1 (Bangalore), applying factory-specific lead times normalized 17 SKUs from false reorder alerts and cut emergency stockout alarms by 80% (from 15 to 3).
  • Executive Presentation & Rollout: Formally presented the Power BI dashboard and inventory tools to the CEO and Supply Chain Manager, with all solutions approved for immediate network-wide operational deployment.

Reflection

This project reinforced that effective supply chain analytics requires balancing technical precision with practical operational usability. While the core calculations were engineered using Python (Pandas, NumPy) and SQL, delivering the final tool in an automated Excel VBA format ensured seamless adoption by depot operations teams. Furthermore, integrating logarithmic volume scaling proved crucial in creating a fair, realistic carrier benchmarking model that reflected real-world transport capacity.
